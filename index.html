About & Technical Information

Overview

HydroGlobal v4 is a browser-based watershed simulator. From one outlet point it delineates the basin and divides it into sub-basins built from MERIT-Basins catchments, retrieves climate, terrain, soil and land-cover data for each sub-basin from open services, derives a-priori parameters for every sub-basin and land-cover unit (HRU), runs a semi-distributed daily SWAT-type water-balance model with routing between sub-basins, and calibrates it against observed discharge where a record is available. In split-sample tests on 20 gauged basins with observed forcing, the engine reached a median validation KGE of 0.86; with live data in six US basins, sub-basin and lumped runs reached median validation KGEs of 0.77 and 0.71, and the sub-basin model was much better at interior gauges where precipitation and snowmelt vary strongly within the basin.

All computation happens locally in your browser. Data is fetched on demand from public providers listed below.

Model Structure

Surface runoffSCS curve number with retention varying continuously with soil water (SWAT soil-moisture method); saturation excess
EvapotranspirationFAO-56 Penman–Monteith reference ET from ERA5, Priestley–Taylor (measured shortwave), or Hargreaves–Samani; canopy interception; soil-water limited uptake
SnowUp to 5 equal-area elevation bands from the DEM, temperature lapse, rain/snow transition, seasonal degree-day melt, sublimation
Soil waterRoot-zone store sized from SoilGrids AWC and land-cover rooting depth; storage-routing percolation and lateral flow
GroundwaterDelayed recharge; shallow aquifer (α recession); slow deep aquifer with an optional loss fraction
RoutingSWAT surface-runoff lag, then a two-reservoir cascade (or Muskingum) scaled by basin area
SedimentMUSLE on a representative hillslope with a delivery ratio — indicative only
Spin-upThe first year is run twice before the record starts; the first 90 days are excluded from scores

Every run reports water-balance closure on the routed outflow (P = ET + Q + loss + ΔStorage, including snow, soil, aquifer and channel storage). It closes to rounding error.

Live Data Sources

Watershed boundaryMERIT-Hydro / HydroSHEDS via the Global Watersheds API (mghydro.com); HydroBASINS and USGS NLDI fallbacks
ClimateERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo: precipitation, Tmax/Tmin, FAO-56 ET₀, snowfall water equivalent, shortwave radiation — mean of points inside the basin
ElevationCopernicus GLO-90 DEM via the Open-Meteo elevation API — up to 90 points inside the basin (hypsometric curve)
SoilISRIC SoilGrids v2, 0–100 cm depth-weighted: texture, bulk density, field capacity, wilting point; Ksat by Saxton et al. (1986)
Land coverMODIS MCD12Q1 IGBP classes (2022) via ORNL DAAC, sampled inside the basin
DischargeUSGS NWIS daily values when a gauge matches the outlet and drainage area (USA); your own CSV anywhere; otherwise a synthetic reference
Geocoding / mapsNominatim (OpenStreetMap); OpenStreetMap, Esri and OpenTopoMap tiles

Calibration & Uncertainty

SearchDDS (Tolson & Shoemaker 2007), particle swarm, or Latin-hypercube Monte Carlo (GLUE)
ObjectivesKGE (2012 form), KGE+logNSE, NSE, logNSE, NSE+PBIAS, R² — days without observations are excluded
Validation70/30 split in time on the observed record; validation scores reported separately
Uncertainty95PPU from the behavioural runs, with P-factor and R-factor computed from that band
SensitivityMultiple regression of the objective on all sampled parameter sets (t-statistic, p-value)
SignaturesBFI, Richards–Baker flashiness, runoff ratio, FDC slope, pulse counts, Q5/Q50/Q95
ExtremesGumbel (EV1) flood quantiles; 7Q10 and deficit analysis on calendar years
ClimatePrecipitation and temperature elasticity of streamflow (Sankarasubramanian et al. 2001)
TrendsMann–Kendall test and Sen slope on annual mean, peak and minimum flow

Reference Discharge & Limitations

Scores only mean something against observations. Where no gauge record is found or uploaded, the app builds a synthetic reference: the long-term runoff volume comes from the Budyko–Fu curve and the daily pattern from this same model with different parameters. Calibrating to it tests internal consistency only, and the interface labels it as synthetic everywhere. Upload a gauge record (GRDC, national services) for any real application.

Other limits: the soil and aquifer are lumped, so strongly heterogeneous or regulated basins (dams, irrigation, large lakes) are poorly represented; ERA5 precipitation can be biased in mountains and the tropics; very large basins are routed with a simple storage cascade. A status badge turns red, and a warning is shown, whenever a data layer could not be retrieved and was estimated.

Help — How to Use

Quick Start (4 steps)

1
Pick a watershed. Click anywhere on the map, search a river/city/basin, type coordinates, or hit a benchmark basin (Amazon, Rhine, …).
2
Fetch Real Data. Press ⬇ Fetch Real Data. The app delineates the basin and pulls ERA5 climate, the DEM, SoilGrids and MODIS land cover, looks for a matching USGS gauge, and sets a-priori parameters. Green badges are live data; a red badge means that layer was estimated.
3
Run the simulation. Choose period & ET method, then press ▶ Run Simulation. Streamflow, water balance and metrics animate in.
4
Calibrate & export. Open Calibration to auto-tune parameters, view Results for diagnostics, and Export to download daily and monthly outputs. Outside the USA, load your own gauge record with ⬆ Load observed discharge before calibrating.

The Tabs

SimulationMain screen — pick location, set parameters, run the model, see live charts
CalibrationAuto-tune parameters (DDS / PSO / GLUE), set ranges, watch dotty plots and sensitivity, get calibration and validation scores
ResultsMonthly sim-vs-obs, flow-duration curve, annual water balance, obs-vs-sim scatter
AnalysisPredictive uncertainty band, hydrological signatures, climate elasticity, flood return periods, drought/low-flow, Mann-Kendall trends
ExportDaily output CSV, monthly reach / land-phase / basin tables, forcing and basin-property CSVs, parameter and performance report, 95PPU band — one file or all as a ZIP

Reading the Metrics

NSENash-Sutcliffe. >0.75 very good · 0.5–0.75 satisfactory · <0 worse than the mean
KGEKling-Gupta. 1 = perfect; balances correlation, bias & variability
logNSENSE on log-flows — rewards low-flow / baseflow accuracy
PBIAS% volume bias. |PBIAS|<10 very good; + means model under-predicts
P-factorFraction of observations inside the 95PPU band (aim >0.7)
R-factorBand width ÷ obs std-dev (aim <1.5 — tight but realistic)

Tips & Troubleshooting

›
Place the outlet on the river you want — delineation snaps to that point, so a tributary click gives a small sub-basin.
›
A red status badge means that data layer could not be retrieved and was estimated. Treat such a run as indicative and reload to retry.
›
Large basins (>50,000 km²) delineate at lower precision and may take 10–20 s on first request.
›
Adjust any parameter slider manually, then re-run — or let Calibration find the best set for you.
›
Switch base maps (🌑 dark / 🛰 satellite / 🏔 terrain) and toggle the basin outline (🌀) from the map controls.
